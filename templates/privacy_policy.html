Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to MediMitra ("we", "our", or "the Platform"). MediMitra is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your sensitive health data with the utmost security and integrity. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and safeguarding in compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play Health Content & Services Policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide medication safety, schedule adherence, and AI-assisted transcription, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, role (Patient, Doctor, Caregiver, Pharmacist), and phone number.
  • Medical & Health Data: Prescribed medicine names, schedules, dosage timings, adherence logs (taken/skipped), allergies, and emergency contact details.
  • Images & Prescriptions: Medicine strip photos and prescription documents captured via camera or uploaded for optical character recognition (OCR) and doctor review.
  • Audio Data (Optional): Voice inquiries to the AI assistant processed temporarily via browser Web Speech API / Android Text-To-Speech.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Generating personalized medicine schedules and dose alerts.
  • Transcribing prescription details for verified doctor review.
  • Sending emergency SOS notifications to your designated family contacts.
  • Checking duplicate medications and adverse drug interactions.

Zero Commercial Sale of Data: MediMitra does NOT sell, rent, or monetize your health data, personal identifiers, or uploaded prescriptions to advertisers, pharmaceutical corporations, or data brokers.

3. Clinical Human Verification & AI Safeguards

MediMitra utilizes artificial intelligence for assistive text extraction only. AI extractions never automatically become active medical prescriptions. All uploaded prescriptions require explicit clinical confirmation by a verified licensed doctor before incorporation into patient regimens.

4. Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard cryptographic protection, including HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit and PBKDF2 password hashing with unique cryptographic salts. Access controls are strictly enforced across Patient, Doctor, Caregiver, and Admin boundaries.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You retain full rights to your medical data. You may review, modify, or permanently delete your account, prescription history, and medication records at any time by contacting our support team or navigating to Settings.
